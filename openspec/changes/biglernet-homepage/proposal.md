## Why

BiglerNet needs a professional homepage to showcase their software engineering and IT solutions services. This will serve as the primary online presence for the company, helping to attract new clients and provide information about their offerings.

## What Changes

- Create a new homepage for BiglerNet with the following pages:
  - Landing page featuring services offered
  - Professional services purchase page
- Modern, professional design that reflects the company's brand
- Responsive layout for mobile and desktop users

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `landing-page`: The main homepage with overview of services, company information, and calls to action
- `services-page`: Dedicated page to display and purchase professional services offered by BiglerNet

### Modified Capabilities
- None

## Impact

- New website structure with multiple pages
- No existing code affected (new project)
- Will require hosting infrastructure for deployment
