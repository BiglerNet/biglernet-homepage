Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BiglerNet ("we", "us") handles information when you visit www.biglernet.com (the "Site"). We keep it simple: we respect your privacy, and we do not sell your data.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Usage and analytics data: information about how visitors use the Site, such as pages viewed, referring website, approximate location (derived from IP address), browser and device type, operating system, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Server logs: our servers and hosting infrastructure automatically record technical information such as IP address, requested URL, and user agent.
  • Information you send us: if you contact us by email or through a contact form, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, and message.

How We Use Information

  • To understand who visits the Site and how it is used, so we can improve it.
  • To keep the Site secure, reliable, and free from abuse.
  • To respond to your inquiries.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Site, such as hosting and analytics providers, and only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the Site.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Analytics tools we use may set cookies or use similar technologies to recognize repeat visits and measure usage. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and many browsers offer a "Do Not Track" or similar privacy option.

Data Retention

We keep analytics and log data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, and correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including serving the Site over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Third-Party Links

The Site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
